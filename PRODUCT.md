# Nishabh Polke

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML generated from shared JavaScript templates, CSS, and progressive JavaScript enhancements. Public GitHub repository and GitHub Pages, serving `/docs` from `main`, as approved in the implementation plan.

## Users

Research hiring teams evaluating Nishabh's methods, contribution, and evidence across community, clinical, and digital-product work.

## Product Purpose

Make four research projects readable as web case studies, retain original work and downloadable portfolios, and provide direct contact details.

## Brand Commitments

Nishabh Polke is the published name. The user specified black and white, Apple-like minimalism, generous spacing, system sans-serif typography, compact navigation, restrained motion, and original colors in project imagery. Joel Bobby is the publishing precedent. These decisions were explicitly approved before implementation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Home, four case studies, and About. Local assets, accessible image enlargement, original PDF downloads, email and LinkedIn contact. No analytics, accounts, contact forms, invented résumé, availability, client names, or business-impact claims.

## Evidence on Hand

GSA 2024 project showcase and gallery; 17-page UX portfolio with smart-home, tobacco-cessation and endoscopic-device studies; 20-page architecture portfolio; FACT recommendation; Scarlet Scout co-authored with Sravani Mummidi; Forres Gazette coverage. The PDFs and public project descriptions are the factual authority. The later Forres event is attributed to the newspaper, not presented as an event Nishabh personally delivered.

## Product Principles

- Lead with research and individual contribution.
- Let original artifacts support readable explanations.
- Separate proposals, delivered artifacts, and later reported developments.
- Preserve source files, credits, and anonymization.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, readable HTML without JavaScript, descriptive links, contrast, and responsive diagrams with enlargement.
