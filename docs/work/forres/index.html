The question

How could local groups and design students build lasting connections in Forres?

Forres had active community groups, but conversations with local leaders suggested that many worked separately. My master’s project explored how to bring those groups into dialogue with Glasgow School of Art students.

The work combined a proposed community event with a tangible engagement tool that stakeholders could continue using.

My contribution

I developed the research, made engagement tools, facilitated co-design, and refined a puzzle and guide for Forres Area Community Trust (FACT).

Co-design · Stakeholder mapping · Research through making

The approach

Make the research tangible

I analyzed primary data through sketches and themes, then used making to reflect on my own relationship with Forres.

Invite other perspectives

A place-based puzzle and guide turned research questions into a co-design activity. Stakeholders used the pieces and map to share experiences and discuss local challenges.

Develop the proposal together

Stakeholder mapping, storyboards, and feedback helped shape the event concept and explore an ongoing relationship between FACT and GSA.

What the research surfaced

A tool could have a life beyond the workshop

Stakeholders saw uses for the puzzle in future community and trustee conversations. I refined the graphics and guide in response.

The research made the process easier to discuss

In her recommendation, Ruth Jeffries describes how the mapping tools helped participants explore the opportunities and practical challenges of collaboration.

Where it led

I handed the finished puzzle and guide to FACT. The event formats and long-term engagement plan were proposals at the time of the showcase.

The Forres Gazette later reported that a community marketplace was inspired by this project. That subsequent event was organized by FACT and GSA; it is separate from my documented handover.

“open and easy to speak with and also well prepared with resources and tools”

Ruth JeffriesBefriending Project Co-Ordinator, FACT · August 2024
