I’m Nishabh.
I work with people.

An architect who moved into UX research and interaction design.

My work has taken me into people’s homes, operating theatres, and community spaces. I use observation, conversation, sketches, and making to understand how people experience the world around them.

At Monsoonfish, I researched connected homes, tobacco-cessation applications, and an endoscopic device. At the Glasgow School of Art, I explored how design could bring local communities and students into conversation.

I carried a smart bulb
across six cities.

During a six-week smart-home study, I travelled from home to home with a bulb in my backpack. It let participants try realistic setup and control tasks.

I noticed how quickly uncertainty could interrupt someone’s progress. People sometimes skipped guidance, tried to tap illustrations, or paused to seek reassurance. Those moments shaped how I think about confidence and accessibility in everyday technology.

How I work
