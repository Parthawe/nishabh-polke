Curiosity, across contexts.

I’m Nishabh Polke, a UX researcher with a background in architecture and interaction design.

My work spans community engagement, digital-product usability, and clinical workflows. Across these settings, I use observation, conversation, and making to understand people’s experiences and turn research into something a team can act on.

I studied MDes Design Innovation & Interaction Design at the Glasgow School of Art. My master’s project explored community connection in Forres, Scotland, through co-design with local stakeholders.

At Monsoonfish, my research included smart-home and tobacco-cessation applications, alongside formative research for an endoscopic device.

How I work

Understand the context

In-home testing, clinical observation, and community conversations bring research into the places where experiences happen.

Make the findings discussable

Sketches, workflow maps, user stories, and engagement tools give teams something concrete to question and work with.

Work with other perspectives

Interviews and co-design make room for the people who use, deliver, or are affected by a service.
